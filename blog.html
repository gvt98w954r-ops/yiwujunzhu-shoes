By Orion | Yiwu Junzhu Shoes.com | July 2026

1. Memory Foam Is Now Standard

In 2026, memory foam is no longer a premium feature — it has become the baseline expectation. Every top-selling slipper on Amazon — from EverFoams (#1, 12,491 reviews) to Evshine (#3, 17,183 reviews) — features memory foam insoles. Buyers now expect "cloud-like comfort" as a standard, not an upgrade. For wholesale suppliers, this means: if your cotton slippers do not have memory foam, they are already behind the market.

2. Cross Band Open Toe Dominates

The fuzzy cross band open toe design is the undisputed #1 style of 2026. This style appeals across all markets — North America, Europe, and the Middle East. Key selling points: breathable, easy slip-on, and visually appealing in product photos. At Yiwu Junzhu, our Fuzzy Cross Band Memory Foam Slippers have sold 3,132 pairs with an MOQ of just 20 pairs.

3. Corduroy & Chenille: The New Materials

While faux fur remains popular, two new materials are surging: corduroy and chenille knit. Amazon listings for corduroy cross band slippers (e.g., Chantomoo, GOEWY) are climbing fast, and Dearfoams chenille slippers have accumulated 41,733 reviews. These materials photograph beautifully and appeal to buyers seeking a "premium but affordable" aesthetic.

4. Indoor/Outdoor Versatility

Buyers increasingly want slippers that work both indoors and outdoors. A rubber or TPR anti-slip sole is now a must-have feature. Products labeled "indoor outdoor" consistently outperform single-use "bedroom only" styles.

5. EVA Cloud Slides: 60,000+ Reviews

The BRONAX Pillow Slippers (#2 on Amazon, 60,544 reviews) prove that EVA cloud platform slides are not just a summer trend — they are a year-round category. Lightweight, waterproof, and extremely low production cost (¥3-5 on 1688), these are high-margin products for wholesale buyers.

6. Small MOQ Is the Competitive Edge

The biggest shift in B2B footwear is the demand for flexible minimum orders. Large factories require 500-1000 pairs minimum, but small-to-medium buyers need 20-50 pairs to test new markets.
