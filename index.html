Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)?

Our standard MOQ is 20 pairs per style, with mixing of colors and sizes allowed. For OEM/custom designs, MOQ may vary based on complexity — typically starting from 500 pairs.

Can I get samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes! We offer free samples for in-stock styles (buyer covers shipping cost). Custom samples are available at production cost. Samples typically ship within 3 business days.

Do you support OEM / custom designs?

Absolutely. We support custom logo, color, material selection, and packaging design. Send us your design file or reference image and we'll provide a quote within 24 hours.

What is your delivery time?

In-stock styles ship within 48 hours of order confirmation. Made-to-order styles typically take 15-25 days depending on quantity and customization requirements.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Trade Assurance (Alibaba Secure Payment), T/T (bank transfer), and L/C. All transactions on Alibaba.com are protected by Trade Assurance.
